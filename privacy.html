Routine

Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Routine is developed by Gregory Richard, Zurich, Switzerland ("we"). This policy explains what data the Routine app collects, why, and your rights under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP) and, where it applies, the EU GDPR.

What we collect

  • Account: your Sign in with Apple identifier and, if you share it, your email address.
  • Training setup: your goal, available days, activity preferences, equipment, body data you enter (such as age, height, weight) and areas of pain or injury you report.
  • Training data: planned sessions, logged sets and weights, check-ins (effort, enjoyment, pain) and messages you send to the coach.
  • Connected services, only if you connect them: Apple Health (workouts, heart rate, body measurements), Garmin Connect (activities including GPS routes and heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep) and Speediance (workout history from your machine).

Why we use it

Only to provide the app: to build and adapt your training plan, send workouts to your devices, and show your results and progress. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for marketing.

Who processes it

  • Supabase (database and server functions, hosted in the EU) stores your data.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) generates your plans and coach replies. It receives the summaries of your training needed for each request. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models.
  • Garmin, Apple and Speediance exchange data with the app only when you connect them, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Disconnecting a service stops further syncing. Deleting your account removes all your data from our systems within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data at any time, and withdraw consent for any connected service. Contact us at richard.gregory.95@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or your local data protection authority.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and tell you in the app about significant changes.
